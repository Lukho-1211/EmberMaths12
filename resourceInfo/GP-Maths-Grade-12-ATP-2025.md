### Week 8: 26/5 – 30/5 (5 days)

4. Using the definition (first principle), determine the derivative, \(f'(x)\) where a, b and c are constants:
   - 4.1 \(f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c\)
   - 4.2 \(f(x) = ax^3\)
   - 4.3 \(f(x) = a/x\)
   - 4.4 \(f(x) = c\)

5. Use the formula \(\frac{d}{dx}(ax^n) = anx^{n-1}\) (for any real number n) together with the rules:
   - 5.1 \(\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) \pm g(x)] = \frac{d}{dx}[f(x)] \pm \frac{d}{dx}[g(x)]\)
   - 5.2 \(\frac{d}{dx}[kf(x)] = k\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)]\) (k a constant)

**Differential Calculus: first principles and rules of differentiation**

6. Determine equations of tangents to graphs of functions.
7. Introduce the second derivative \(f''(x) = \frac{d}{dx}(f'(x))\) of \(f(x)\) and how it determines the concavity of a function.
8. Sketch graphs of cubic polynomial functions using differentiation to determine the coordinates of stationary points, and points of inflection (where concavity changes). Also, determine the x-intercepts of the graph using the factor theorem and other techniques.
9. Interpret sketched cubic functions including the graph of the derivative of a cubic function.
